Privacy

The Overwatch mobile apps collect nothing.

No account exists. No analytics, no advertising, no trackers, no identifiers. The app requests public, anonymous data from the Overwatch API (overwatch.confinia.io) — satellite positions and the open observation statistics of the SatNOGS volunteer network — and stores exactly one thing on your device: the name of the station you chose to follow.

If you enable a quiet-station alert, your device's push subscription is stored so the alert can reach you, and deleted the moment you turn it off or uninstall.
