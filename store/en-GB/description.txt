Overwatch answers the one question every satellite ground-station operator has: is my station okay?

Pick your station once. From then on the app opens straight onto it: your reception rate compared against your own history — never an absolute number, because a station that tracks different satellites is not a worse station — with a three-week sparkline and a plain verdict: hearing as usual, or below your baseline.

DRILL INTO EVERY PASS
Each recent pass shows the frames your station decoded during it. Open a pass for the frame-by-frame timeline: ticks spread across the whole window mean a healthy chain; ticks clustered around the highest elevation suggest a horizon problem. Tap any frame to see every decoded value — battery voltages, temperatures, spacecraft mode.

NEXT PASSES
Rise times and peak elevation for the tracked fleet over your station, so you know when to listen.

THE SATELLITES
Live positions, altitude, eclipse state and last-heard times for the fleet, drawn from the SatNOGS network and CelesTrak orbital elements. Tap any satellite for its card; the full web control room at overwatch.confinia.io is one link away when you want the globe and the deep dashboards.

BUILT ON OPEN DATA
Everything in the app is computed from the open observations of the SatNOGS volunteer ground-station network. Overwatch sees your station only through the satellites it tracks — the app says so rather than letting you discover it.

No account. No ads. No trackers. Open source.
